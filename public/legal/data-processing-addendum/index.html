Data Processing Addendum

Last updated 14 October 2026

1. Scope and roles

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") forms part of the Terms of Service between Compnet Systems ("Processor") and the customer organization ("Controller"). It applies to personal data contained in Customer Data that we process on the Controller's behalf in providing the Service. Terms such as "personal data", "processing", "controller", "processor" and "data subject" have the meanings in the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 ("NDPA") and, where applicable, the GDPR.

By subscribing to the Service the Controller accepts this DPA. No separate signature is required; a countersigned copy is available on request to legal@firmtracks.com.

2. Processing instructions

We will process personal data only on the Controller's documented instructions, which are the Terms, this DPA, and the Controller's use of the Service's features. If we believe an instruction breaches the law we will tell you. Details of processing are in the Annex.

3. Confidentiality

We ensure that personnel authorized to process personal data are bound by confidentiality obligations and have access only as needed for their duties.

4. Security

We maintain technical and organizational measures appropriate to the risk, described in Security and Sub-processors. We may update these measures provided overall protection is not materially reduced.

5. Sub-processors

The Controller gives general authorization for us to engage the sub-processors listed on the Security and Sub-processors page. We bind each to data protection obligations no less protective than this DPA and remain responsible for their performance. We will give at least 30 days' notice of new or replacement sub-processors, by email to account administrators or by updating that page. The Controller may object on reasonable data protection grounds within that period; if we cannot resolve the objection, the Controller may terminate the affected Service with a pro-rata refund of prepaid fees.

6. Assistance with data subject rights

Taking into account the nature of processing, we will help the Controller respond to requests from data subjects to exercise their rights, using the export, correction and deletion tools in the Service where possible. If a data subject contacts us directly about Customer Data, we will refer them to the Controller.

7. Personal data breaches

We will notify the Controller without undue delay, and in any event within 72 hours, after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting Customer Data, with the information then available about the nature of the breach, likely consequences and measures taken, and will keep the Controller updated. The Controller is responsible for any notification it must make to the NDPC or data subjects, and we will help as reasonably needed.

8. International transfers

The Controller authorizes processing in the locations used by our sub-processors. Where personal data is transferred out of Nigeria or the EEA/UK, we will ensure an appropriate transfer mechanism is in place as required by the NDPA or GDPR, such as standard contractual clauses, which are incorporated by reference where needed.

9. Audits and information

We will make available information reasonably needed to show compliance with this DPA and allow for audits by the Controller or its auditor, no more than once a year (or after a breach), on reasonable notice, during business hours, and subject to confidentiality. We may satisfy audit requests first by providing third-party reports or certifications where available.

10. Return and deletion

On termination the Controller may export Customer Data for 30 days. After that we will delete or anonymize it, and any remaining copies in backups on the schedule in the Terms, unless the law requires retention.

11. Liability and order of precedence

Each party's liability under this DPA is subject to the limitations in the Terms. If this DPA conflicts with the Terms on data protection matters, this DPA prevails.
