Cookie Policy

Last updated 14 October 2026

1. What are cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We also use similar technologies such as local storage. They help the site remember you, keep you signed in, and understand how the Service is used.

2. How we use them

CategoryPurposeCan you refuse?
EssentialSign-in sessions, security, load balancing, remembering your workspace and cookie choiceNo (the Service cannot work without them)
PreferencesLanguage, theme, table and layout settingsYes
AnalyticsAggregate understanding of page and feature use. To confirm: name the analytics tool, or delete this row if none is usedYes
MarketingMeasuring marketing campaigns on firmtracks.com. To confirm: delete if no marketing pixels are usedYes

3. Your choices

You can set cookie preferences through the banner on our website and change them at any time, and you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies will stop sign-in from working.

4. Third parties

Microsoft sets cookies when you sign in with your work account, under Microsoft's own policies. Paystack may set cookies on its payment pages.

5. Contact

Questions: privacy@firmtracks.com. See also the Privacy Policy.
