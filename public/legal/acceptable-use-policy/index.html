Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated 14 October 2026

1. Purpose

This policy applies to everyone who uses FirmTrack, including administrators, staff, contractors and API integrations. It supports the Terms of Service.

2. You must

  • Use the Service lawfully and in line with your professional and regulatory duties.
  • Keep credentials and access tokens secure and promptly remove access for people who leave.
  • Give staff clear notice before enabling any feature that records or infers their activity, such as the TimeTrack desktop agent, and meet your obligations under employment and data protection law.

3. You must not

  • Break the law, infringe others' rights, or upload content you have no right to use.
  • Upload malware, or attempt to disrupt, overload, probe or bypass security or tenant-isolation controls.
  • Access, or try to access, another organization's workspace or data.
  • Reverse engineer, scrape or copy the Service, or use it to build a competing product.
  • Resell or share seats, or use one user account for several people.
  • Use the Service to send spam, to phish, or to impersonate others.
  • Covertly monitor individuals, or use activity data for purposes unlawful in your jurisdiction.
  • Exceed reasonable API or automated-request limits, or use automation to degrade the Service for others.

4. Security testing and vulnerability reports

Please do not run security tests against production without our written permission. If you find a vulnerability, report it to security@firmtracks.com with enough detail to reproduce it. We will acknowledge good-faith reports, will not pursue legal action against researchers who follow this policy, avoid privacy harm and service disruption, and give us reasonable time to fix the issue before disclosure.

5. Enforcement

If we believe this policy is breached we may investigate, remove content, throttle or suspend access, or terminate the account. We will give notice where practicable, except where the risk is urgent or the law requires immediate action. We may report unlawful activity to the authorities.

6. Reporting abuse

Email abuse@firmtracks.com.
