Subscription, Billing and Refund Policy

Last updated 14 October 2026

1. Plans and seats

FirmTrack is billed per user, per month, by module and plan tier (Free, Basic, Standard or Elite). Your workspace's billing page shows the modules, seat count and price for each billing period. Adding seats during a period is charged pro rata for the remainder of that period. To confirm: confirm whether seat reductions apply immediately or at the next renewal

2. Currency, payment and taxes

  • Prices are shown in Nigerian naira (NGN) unless the checkout shows another currency. To confirm: add USD pricing text once multi-currency is live
  • Payments are processed by Paystack. We do not store full card numbers.
  • Prices exclude VAT and other applicable taxes unless stated. Tax is added where required.
  • Where a USD equivalent is shown, it is for information; the amount charged is the amount shown at checkout.

3. Renewal

Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each monthly or annual period at the then-current price. We will tell you by email at least To confirm: e.g. 30 days before a price change takes effect on your subscription, and annual subscribers before renewal.

Annual plans are discounted compared with paying monthly.

4. Failed payments

If a payment fails we will retry and email your billing administrators. If it remains unpaid after To confirm: grace period, e.g. 14 days, we may restrict access to read-only and then suspend the workspace. Your data is not deleted because of non-payment during the first To confirm: e.g. 60 days after suspension, and you can reactivate by paying outstanding fees.

5. Cancellation

You may cancel at any time from billing settings. Cancellation stops future renewals. You keep access until the end of the paid period, and you can export your data for 30 days afterwards as set out in the Terms.

6. Refunds

  • Monthly plans: fees are not refunded for a period already begun, except where required by law or where we have materially failed to provide the Service.
  • First annual subscription: you may request a full refund within To confirm: e.g. 14 days of first purchase of an annual plan.
  • Annual renewals: you may request a refund within To confirm: e.g. 7 days after an automatic renewal if you have not used the Service in the new period.
  • Our termination without cause, or removal of a paid feature: pro-rata refund of prepaid fees for the period after termination.
  • No refunds for seats or modules that were used, for taxes already remitted where the tax authority does not refund them, or for suspension or termination for breach of the Terms.

Refunds go back to the original payment method through Paystack where possible, and can take several business days to appear. Request a refund at billing@firmtracks.com with your workspace name and the payment reference.

7. Disputes

Please contact us before raising a chargeback so we can fix the issue quickly. We may suspend a workspace while a chargeback is investigated.
