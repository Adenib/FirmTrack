Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 October 2026

1. Who we are

Compnet Systems ("FirmTrack", "we") operates the FirmTrack platform. This policy explains how we handle personal data in two roles:

  • As controller of data about visitors to our website, people who contact us, and the account and billing details of our customers.
  • As processor of the data your firm puts into the Service (clients, matters, time entries, staff records and so on). For that data, your firm is the controller and the Data Processing Addendum applies. If you are a client or employee of a firm using FirmTrack, please contact that firm about your data.

2. Data we collect

  • Account and sign-in data: name, work email, job title, Microsoft Entra object ID, tenant ID, role and workspace membership. We receive these from Microsoft when you sign in. We never see or store your Microsoft password.
  • Customer Data entered by firms: client and matter records, lawyer details and billing rates, time entries, tasks, calendar bookings, leave and movement records, documents and invoices, as configured by your firm.
  • TimeTrack desktop agent data: if your firm enables the agent, the application name and window title of the active window and timing information, used to propose draft time entries. See the TimeTrack Desktop Agent Notice.
  • Billing data: plan, seat count, invoices and payment status. Card and bank details are collected and held by Paystack, not by us.
  • Usage and technical data: log-in times, IP address, device and browser type, error logs and audit trails of actions in the Service.
  • Communications: messages you send to support or sales, and marketing preferences.

3. How we use data

  • To provide, secure, support and improve the Service, including authentication and tenant isolation.
  • To bill and manage subscriptions, and to prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To communicate service notices, security alerts and, where permitted, product updates (you can opt out of marketing at any time).
  • To meet legal obligations and to establish or defend legal claims.

We do not sell personal data and do not use Customer Data for advertising.

4. Legal bases

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and, where it applies, the GDPR, we rely on: performance of our contract with you; our legitimate interests in running and securing a business service (balanced against your rights); compliance with legal obligations; and consent where we ask for it (for example, optional cookies or marketing emails). Your firm is responsible for the legal basis for the Customer Data it processes.

5. Who we share data with

We share personal data only with service providers that help us run FirmTrack, under written agreements, as listed on our Security and Sub-processors page (currently including Supabase for database and authentication services, Paystack for payments, and Microsoft for sign-in). We may also disclose data to professional advisers, to a successor in a merger or sale of the business (with notice), and to authorities where required by law or court order.

6. International transfers

Our providers may process data outside Nigeria. Where personal data leaves Nigeria (or the EEA/UK for GDPR-covered data), we use safeguards recognised by law, such as contractual protections and adequacy decisions, as required by the NDPA and GDPR. Our primary data region is To confirm: Supabase project region and hosting region.

7. Retention

  • Customer Data: kept while your subscription is active, and deleted after the post-termination export window described in the Terms.
  • Account and billing records: kept for as long as needed for the relationship and then for the period required by tax and accounting law. To confirm: retention period, e.g. 6 years
  • Logs and audit trails: kept for To confirm: e.g. 12 months unless needed for an investigation.
  • Marketing contacts: until you unsubscribe.

8. Security

We use technical and organizational measures designed for a multi-tenant platform, including encryption in transit, tenant isolation using row-level security, server-side handling of secrets, and role-based access. See Security and Sub-processors. No system is perfectly secure, and we will notify affected customers and, where required, the regulator of personal data breaches as the law requires.

9. Your rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have the right to access your personal data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to processing, receive it in a portable format, withdraw consent, and not be subject to solely automated decisions with significant effects. To exercise a right, email privacy@firmtracks.com. If your data is held by a firm that uses FirmTrack, we may refer your request to that firm.

You may complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) or, if you are in the EEA or UK, to your local supervisory authority.

10. Children

The Service is for professional use and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18 for our own purposes.
