TimeTrack Desktop Agent Notice

Last updated 14 October 2026

1. What the agent is

The TimeTrack desktop agent is an optional Windows application. When enabled by your firm and installed by a user, it notes which application and window are active, for how long, and uses that to propose draft time entries matched to matters. A person always reviews and accepts, edits or discards a draft before it becomes a time entry.

2. What it captures

  • The name of the active application and the title of the active window.
  • Start and end times and duration of activity, and idle periods.
  • The user and workspace the activity belongs to.

Window titles can include document names, matter names or email subjects, so they may contain confidential or personal information. For that reason, they are treated as Customer Data. To confirm: describe exactly what the agent sends to the server versus what stays on the device

3. What it does not capture

To confirm: only keep each line below if it is true of the shipped agent The agent does not record keystrokes, take screenshots, read the contents of documents or emails, record audio or video, or track location.

4. Who controls it

  • Your firm decides whether to enable the agent, which users it applies to, and what happens with the data. The firm is the controller and must have a lawful basis and give staff proper notice.
  • Users can see what has been captured in their draft entries, and can pause the agent from the system tray. To confirm: pause/ pause-per-app / exclusion list features
  • Not for covert monitoring. The Acceptable Use Policy prohibits using the agent to monitor people secretly or unlawfully.

5. Where data goes and how long it is kept

Agent data is sent over an encrypted connection to your firm's workspace and stored with the rest of its Customer Data. Raw activity records are kept for To confirm: e.g. 30 days after the draft is accepted or discarded, then deleted. Accepted time entries are kept under your firm's retention settings.

6. Staff notice (for firms to adapt)

Firms may adapt the following for their own staff policy: "We use the FirmTrack TimeTrack agent to help record billable time. It notes which applications and windows you use during working hours so it can propose draft time entries for your review. It does not record what you type or take screenshots. You review all drafts before they are used, and you can pause the agent. Contact [HR/IT contact] with questions or to exercise your data protection rights."
