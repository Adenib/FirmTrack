Microsoft 365 Integration and Permissions

Last updated 14 October 2026

1. How FirmTrack connects

FirmTrack is a multi-tenant application registered in Microsoft Entra ID. Users sign in with their work Microsoft account. The first time a person from your organization signs in, Microsoft may require a tenant administrator to review and grant consent for the permissions FirmTrack requests.

2. Permissions we request

3. What we do not do

  • We never see or store your Microsoft password.
  • We do not read email content or files unless your organization grants a permission for it and enables a feature that needs it.
  • We do not use Microsoft data for advertising.

4. Tokens and storage

Access and refresh tokens issued by Microsoft are kept on the server and are not exposed to the browser. To confirm: confirm whether refresh tokens are stored and whether they are encrypted at rest

5. Revoking access

A tenant administrator can remove FirmTrack at any time in the Microsoft Entra admin center under Enterprise applications > FirmTrack > Properties > Delete, or can revoke specific permissions. Users will no longer be able to sign in, and we stop receiving new data from Microsoft. Existing Customer Data stays in your workspace under the Terms until you cancel and the export window passes.

6. Who can approve

Only a Microsoft 365 administrator with the right to consent for the organization can grant tenant-wide approval. Individuals may be able to consent for themselves, depending on your tenant's settings.
